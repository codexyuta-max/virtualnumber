Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Virtual Number Seller handles information when you use our website or open a personal website link. We collect limited technical information to protect the service, investigate abuse, and improve security.

1. Information we collect

  • Your IP address and approximate IP-based location, including country, region, city, timezone, and an approximate map link.
  • Precise location coordinates only if you explicitly allow your browser's location permission prompt.
  • Browser and device information, such as user agent, platform, language, screen resolution, CPU core count, available device memory, and storage estimates.
  • Battery level and charging status when your browser makes that information available.

If location permission is denied or unavailable, only the IP-based approximate location is used.

2. How we use information

We use this information for fraud prevention, abuse detection, troubleshooting, service security, and to operate the personal-link feature. Information from a personal link is sent to the Telegram account associated with that link.

3. Location permission

Your browser controls precise-location permission. You can deny or revoke it in browser settings at any time. If permission is denied, we use only approximate IP-based location where available.

4. Retention and sharing

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, and service operation. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the security of the service and its users.

5. Your choices and contact

If you do not agree with this collection, do not open or continue using a personal website link. For privacy questions, contact the service administrator on Telegram: @its_aadish.
