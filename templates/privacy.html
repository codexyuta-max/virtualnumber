Privacy Policy

This educational application collects browser/device information only after explicit consent.

What We Collect (After Consent)

  • Device model, user agent, language, and screen resolution
  • Public IP and approximate location (country, region, city, timezone)
  • Coordinates when you grant geolocation permission
  • Battery status and camera/location permission states
  • CPU cores, RAM estimate, storage used/total estimate

Optional Telegram Share

  • Telegram sharing is optional and user-triggered only
  • Sharing requires a chat ID, a checkbox confirmation, and a second confirmation click
  • No automatic background Telegram forwarding is performed

What We Do Not Do

  • No camera or microphone stream access
  • No hidden collection beyond the listed fields
  • No permission bypassing

How Data Is Used

Data is displayed to you on the page, sent to this Flask backend via POST, and stored in MongoDB for dashboard display.

Educational use only.
